Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Egenråd is a private tool used only by its owner, Arild Ovesen (arild.ovesen@gmail.com). It has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the app has read-only access to the owner's own Google Workspace data, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Tasks. It does not send email, change calendar events or modify files.

How data is used

Data is read on request to help the owner with personal planning and notes. It is processed on the owner's own computers and by the AI assistant the owner uses. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train models.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own encrypted computers. Nothing is stored on any public server.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
